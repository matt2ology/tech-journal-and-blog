## Key Takeaways & Core Concepts

> [!abstract] Executive Summary & Core Thesis
>
> 1. Ackermann claims and propose using [[../../../notes/courses/generative-ai-for-improving-writing/n03-generative-ai-for-improving-writing-using-ai-before-you-start-writing-adapting-content-for-different-audiences#idTwLFqv046o|GenAi can accelerate the writing process]] without replacing your unique voice or style when [[../../../notes/courses/generative-ai-for-improving-writing/n03-generative-ai-for-improving-writing-using-ai-before-you-start-writing-adapting-content-for-different-audiences#idd3LFLkKLKr|communicating to different audiences]], but she is disingenuous [[../../../notes/courses/generative-ai-for-improving-writing/n03-generative-ai-for-improving-writing-using-ai-before-you-start-writing-adapting-content-for-different-audiences#idp4QiJcJc2g|AI outlining your talking points does impose on and robs one from their unique voice and style]].
> 2. It is important to prioritize human creative control as it is a requirement for regional copyright laws, but she does not detail or guide as to what percentage of human input and AI generation is sufficient to be compliant.
> 3. GenAI can help one write and ideate faster by leveraging both "AI‑assisted" (i.e. modifying the text outputted from GenAI) and "AI‑generated writing" (i.e. copy-and-paste).
> 4. [[../../../notes/courses/generative-ai-for-improving-writing/n03-generative-ai-for-improving-writing-using-ai-before-you-start-writing-adapting-content-for-different-audiences#id08EfSEFGxt|Although she contradicts herself]], Ackmann propose that there are 5 components to making a strong prompt; and, by leveraging her proposed structure will lead to better prompts and by extension better starts.
>    1. **Role:** **w**ho the AI should act as (defines expertise / voice)
>    2. **Task:** **w**hat you want it to do (the main output)
>    3. **Context - why it matters:** background information the **w**ho, **w**hat, **w**hen, **w**hy
>    4. **Structure:** ho**w** you want the output formatted or organized
>    5. **Tone / audience - the output:** **w**ho the audience is and what tone / style you want
> 5. Personal privacy and data security is important, so [[../../../notes/courses/generative-ai-for-improving-writing/n03-generative-ai-for-improving-writing-using-ai-before-you-start-writing-adapting-content-for-different-audiences#idZUPSnInaH8|Ackermann suggest that one should use generics when drafting their prompts]]; again, [[../../../notes/courses/generative-ai-for-improving-writing/n03-generative-ai-for-improving-writing-using-ai-before-you-start-writing-adapting-content-for-different-audiences#id08EfSEFGxt|Ackermann contradicts herself by instructing one to be specific when prompting as general prompts will yield general, not as helpful, outputs]].
