## Summary

- AI gives ignorant people the ability to appear smart as it does what the honest, good faith, hard working people do: research and care
- Prior to AI ignorant people could only leverage the extent of knowledge they were comfortable with being challenged with
- When challenged more than an ignorant person is willing to work though or handle that ignorant person, most often than not, will retract and disengage
- Now with AI - ignorant people feel more powerful, confident, in arguing their lack of critical and rational points of views
- Ignorant people are often easily influenced as they don't validate and trust as long as the statements sounds true to their limited world view
- AI empower ignorant people to present their messy, fuzzy, lazy arguments without the time it takes to apply critical logic and reasoning.
- AI is built for engagement and will praise and encourage bad ideas as good ones
- Enlightenment, understanding, and improving one's self use to be something one would put hard work and care building over time, but now it is prompted
