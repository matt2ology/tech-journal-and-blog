## 🔪 Prep work

- **Season the meat:** Generously coat all sides of the oxtail pieces with salt and freshly ground black pepper.
- **Chop the aromatics:** Dice the sweet onion, chop the scallions, and mince the fresh garlic and ginger.
- **Prep the beans:** Open, drain, and thoroughly rinse the canned butter beans in cold water.
- **Optional - Prep the peppers:** Keep the Scotch bonnet peppers whole, but pierce them once or twice with a knife tip to gently release heat into the gravy during cooking.

## 👩‍🍳 Instructions (Steps)

1. **Sear the Oxtails:** Heat the cooking oil in a large Dutch oven over medium-high heat.
   1. Working in batches so the pot isn't overcrowded, sear the oxtail pieces until deeply browned on all sides.
   2. Transfer the browned meat to a plate and set aside.
2. **Sweat Aromatics & Bloom Spices:** In the same pot with the rendered fat and oil, add the chopped sweet onions and scallions with a light pinch of salt.
   1. Sauté for 2–3 minutes until softened.
   2. Stir in the Jamaican curry powder and paprika, letting the spices toast in the oil for 1 minute until fragrant.
3. **Add Garlic & Ginger:** Stir in the minced garlic and fresh ginger.
   1. Sauté for about 1 minute until fragrant, taking care not to burn the garlic.
4. **Build the Gravy Base:** Stir in the tomato paste, Worcestershire sauce, pimento seeds, browning seasoning, and chicken base.
   1. Mix well until a thick, rich mixture forms.
5. **Simmer the Stew:** Return the seared oxtails and any accumulated resting juices back to the pot.
   1. Add the fresh thyme sprigs and pierced Scotch bonnet peppers.
   2. Pour in enough water or broth to partially submerge the meat.
   3. Bring to a rapid boil, then reduce the heat to low, cover with a tight-fitting lid, and simmer low and slow for about 2 to 3 hours, until the meat is fork-tender and practically falling off the bone.
6. **Add Butter Beans & Finish:** Carefully stir in the rinsed butter beans.
   1. Uncover the pot and simmer on medium-low for another 15 minutes, allowing the beans to absorb the flavors and the rich gravy to thicken.
   2. Discard the thyme stems and whole Scotch bonnet peppers before serving.
