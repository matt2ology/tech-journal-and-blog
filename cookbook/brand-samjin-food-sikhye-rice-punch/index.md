## 💡 Notes & Variations

- 500g of barley malt to make 4 liters (17 cups)

## 🔪 Prep work

- Cook 500g of rice (about 2-3 bowls)
  - 500g ≈ 17.637 Ounce
  - 500g ≈ 1 Pound

## 👩‍🍳 Instructions (Steps)

### Step 1) 15 min Soak

- Pour cold water over 500g and let it soak for about 10 - 20 minutes

### Step 2) discard soak, 4 liters water - sediments settles

- After discarding the soaking water
- Put the the soaked malt into a large bowl
- Pour in 4 liters of water (≈ 17 cups)
- Strain out the barley husks though a sieve
- Wait until the sediments settles

### Step 3) 30 min wait - collect only clear water

- After 30 minutes
- Collect only the clear water and discard the sediment

### Step 4) clear water added to cooked rice - warm setting (5 hours)

- In the rice cooker where the 500g of cooked rice (about 2-3 bowls) was made pour in the clear water
- Add a small amount of sugar
- Let it ferment for 5-8 hours in the rice cooker on the warm setting until the rice grains float to the surface

### Step 5) pour all contents into pot - boil vigorously

- Pour the fermented rice and water into a pot and boil vigorously over a stove

### Step 6) Skim foam, add sugar/ginger, boil for 12 min, store cold

- Skim of the foam that forms, add sugar or ginger to taste - if desired
- Boil for about 10 to 15 minutes, let it cool, and store in the refrigerator
