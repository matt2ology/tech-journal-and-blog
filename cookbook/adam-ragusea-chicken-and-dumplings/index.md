## 🛒 Ingredients

### Main Components

- [ ] 1.5 lbs (700g) boneless, skinless chicken thighs
- [ ] 8 oz (227g) mushrooms (halved or quartered)
- [ ] 2 bunches green onions (whites chopped for soup, greens reserved for garnish)
- [ ] 1 lb carrots (thickly sliced)
- [ ] 1 carton (approx. 4 cups / 1 liter) chicken stock
- [ ] 12 oz (340g) fresh green beans (cut into bite-sized pieces)
- [ ] 1 head garlic (cloves smashed, peel removed)

### Seasonings & Pantry

- [ ] Black pepper
- [ ] Mixed dried herbs
- [ ] Salt
- [ ] All-purpose flour (approx. ½ cup / 50–60g for coating chicken)
- [ ] Cooking oil or bacon grease
- [ ] White wine (approx. 1 bottle, optional; or sub with water/stock)
- [ ] Cake flour (approx. 2 cups / 230g; all-purpose flour works if needed)
- [ ] 2 tsp baking powder
- [ ] ½ tsp garlic powder
- [ ] Fresh rosemary and sage (finely chopped)
- [ ] 1 lemon (zested for dumplings, juiced for finishing)
- [ ] 2 - 3 tbsp butter (melted)
- [ ] Milk or water (approx. 1 cup / 240ml max)
- [ ] 5 - 6 egg yolks
- [ ] Heavy cream (approx. 3 parts cream to 1 part egg yolks, for liaison)

---

## 🔪 Prep work

1. Cut boneless skinless chicken thighs into ~4 pieces each using kitchen shears (don't have to a cutting board)
2. Halve smaller mushrooms, quarter larger mushrooms, and thick-slice the carrots
3. Slice the green onions, separating the white stems from the green tops (reserve green tops for garnish)
4. Smash the garlic cloves and peel off the skins
5. Cut green beans into bite-sized pieces
6. Finely chop the fresh rosemary and sage, and zest/juice the lemon

## 👩‍🍳 Instructions (Steps)

1. Season chicken pieces directly with pepper, mixed dried herbs, and salt
2. Then the season chicken pieces toss with ½ cup (or about 50-60 g) flour to coat evenly
3. In a large pot - on medium-high heat add a good film of oil (or bacon grease)
4. Add chicken pieces one by one (NOT ALL AT ONCE)
5. Sear until browned, then flip
6. Push chicken to one side of the pot and add mushrooms to brown
7. Add green onion whites and sauté for a few minutes
8. Deglaze the pot with white wine (or stock/water), scraping up any browned bits
9. Add carrots, smashed garlic cloves, and chicken stock
10. Season with a pinch of salt, bring to a boil, reduce to a simmer, cover, and cook for 45 minutes
11. Add green beans to the simmering pot and cook for another 15–30 minutes until vegetables and meat are tender
12. Prepare dumpling dough: In a bowl, combine cake flour, baking powder, ½ tsp salt, garlic powder, chopped herbs, and lemon zest
13. **Do not overmix:** stir in melted butter to coat the flour, then mix in just enough milk/water (up to 1 cup) to create a shaggy, barely cohesive dough
14. Gently roll dough into small balls (they double in size) and drop them into the gently boiling soup
15. Cover and steam for 10–15 minutes until cooked through and fluffy
16. Prepare the liaison by whisking together the egg yolks and heavy cream until smooth
17. Turn off or lower heat so there are no active bubbles, then briskly stir in the egg-cream mixture to thicken and enrich the broth without curdling
18. Turn off heat completely, taste and adjust salt, squeeze in lemon juice, and stir in the reserved green onion tops before serving
