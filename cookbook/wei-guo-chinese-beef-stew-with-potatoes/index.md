## Notes & Variations

> [!tip]
>
> To speed up the process, use an instant pot.
>
> If available, use a thick-walled pot with a tight-fitting lid (e.g. a dutch oven).
> This helps to lock in the heat and moisture.

- **Shaoxing rice wine** (1 tablespoon) substitute:
  - Chicken, Mushroom, or Vegetable Stock,
  - Dry Sherry
  - Dry White Wine
  - Mirin
  - Sake

## Ingredients

### Main Components

- [ ] 1x **medium carrot**, cut into chunks, (~120g | 4oz )
- [ ] 2x **onions**, sliced
- [ ] 3x **medium potatoes**, peeled and cut into chunks, (~350g | 12oz)
- [ ] 2.5 cups **hot water** (600 ml)
- [ ] 1.5 lb [beef, cut into chunks](https://www.seriouseats.com/best-cut-beef-stew-braise) (700 g)
  - Bohemian (Bottom Sirloin Flap)
  - Bone-in short rib
  - Chuck
  - Cross-cut shanks
  - Fatty brisket ("point" or "second cut")
  - Oxtail

### Seasonings & Pantry

- [ ] 1x **Cassia cinnamon (Chinese cinnamon)** (1-piece)
- [ ] 1x **Ginger** - thumb-sized, smashed
- [ ] 2x **Star anises** (2-pieces)
- [ ] **Bay leaves** (2-pieces)
- [ ] **Cloves** (2-pieces)
- [ ] **Coriander (cilantro)** - a handful, chopped, optional
- [ ] **Dark soy sauce** (1 tablespoon)

- [ ] **Light soy sauce** (2 tablespoon)
- [ ] **Neutral cooking oil** (2 tablespoon)
- [ ] **Salt** (¼ teaspoon or to taste)
- [ ] **Shaoxing rice wine** (1 tablespoon)

## Prep work

1. 2 **onions**, sliced
2. 1 smash ginger
3. [Beef, cut into chunks](https://www.seriouseats.com/best-cut-beef-stew-braise) (1.5 lb | 700 g)

### Blanch the beef

1. Add beef (cut into chunks) into a pot filled with COLD water then turn the heat to high
2. As the water reaches boiling point, you’ll see some brownish froth appearing on the surface
3. Use a spoon to skim it off
4. Drain the beef - once most of the froth is discarded

## Instructions

### Fry the onion

1. In a pot add 2 tablespoon **neutral cooking oil**
2. Slowly **brown sliced onions** over medium-low heat
3. Stir and fry until fragrant (so to add hint of caramelised sweetness to the dish):
   - Ginger
   - Cassia cinnamon (Chinese cinnamon)
   - Star anises

### Braise the Meat

1. Add to the pot:
   - **Dark soy sauce** (1 tablespoon)
   - Drained [beef chunks](https://www.seriouseats.com/best-cut-beef-stew-braise) (1.5 lb | 700 g)
   - **Light soy sauce** (2 tablespoon)
   - **Shaoxing rice wine** (1 tablespoon)
2. Pour in about 2½ cups (600ml) of hot water
3. Over high heat, bring the water to a full boil
4. Turn the heat down to the lowest
5. **~1½ hours** cover the pot with a lid and leave to simmer **until the beef becomes tender**

### Add the vegetables

1. **Add potato** and **carrot** chunks
2. **10 to 15 minutes** simmer uncovered **until the vegetables become tender**

> [!tip]
> If you wish to serve the dish in less broth, cook a little further over high heat to reduce its volume.
>
> Otherwise, serve it as it is (I think the flavourful broth goes wonderfully well with plain rice).
