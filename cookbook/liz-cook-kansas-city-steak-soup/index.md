**Source:** [Serious Eats: Kansas City Steak Soup by Liz Cook](https://www.seriouseats.com/kansas-city-steak-soup-recipe-11682976)

## Equipment

- Dutch oven or large heavy-bottomed stock pot
- Wooden spoon

## Notes & Variations

- I've added to the soup:
  - Parsnips
  - Spinach
  - Croissants to dip in soup

## Ingredients

### Main Components

- [ ] **Cremini mushrooms** (8 ounces | 227 g), trimmed and thinly sliced
- [ ] **Dried porcini mushrooms** (1/2 ounce | 14 g)
- [ ] **Unsalted butter** (4 tablespoons | 57 g), divided
- [ ] 1/2 Medium **yellow onion** (4 ounces; 110 g), diced
- [ ] 1x Medium **carrot** (4 ounces; 80 g), peeled and diced
- [ ] 2x Small **ribs celery** (80 g), halved lengthwise, then sliced
- [ ] Kansas City strip steak or **NY strip steak** (1 pound | about 2 steaks), trimmed of excess fat and diced into 3/4 inch cubes

### Seasonings & Pantry

- [ ] **All-purpose flour** (1/4 cup | 37 g)
- [ ] **Canned diced tomatoes** (8 ounces | 1 cup), drained
- [ ] **Dry sherry** (1/2 cup | 120 ml), such as oloroso sherry
- [ ] **Low-sodium chicken broth** or [homemade chicken stock](https://www.seriouseats.com/best-rich-easy-white-chicken-stock-recipe) (4 cups | 950 ml)
- [ ] **Tomato paste** (1 tablespoon | 25 g)
- [ ] **Worcestershire sauce** (1 tablespoon | 15 ml)
- [ ] Bottled **steak sauce** (1 tablespoon | 15 ml), preferably A1
- [ ] Diamond Crystal **kosher salt** (1 teaspoon) plus more for seasoning steak; for table salt, use half as much by volume
- [ ] Freshly ground **black pepper** (1 teaspoon), plus more for seasoning steak

## Prep work

- **Cremini mushrooms**, trimmed and thinly sliced
- 1/2 Medium **yellow onion**, diced
- 1x Medium **carrot**, peeled and diced
- 2x Small **ribs celery**, halved lengthwise, then sliced
- Kansas City strip steak or **NY strip steak**, trimmed of excess fat and diced into 3/4 inch cubes

### Dried Porcini Mushrooms

In a microwave-safe container:

1. ~3 minutes on high, heat 4 cups of **chicken stock** and **dried porcini mushrooms** until stock is hot and mushrooms are fully hydrated
2. Use a slotted spoon to transfer hydrated porcini to a cutting board
3. Chop porcini and return to stock
4. Set aside

## Instructions

### Step 1: Sear the Steak

1. **Pat steak dry** and **season steak cubes** generously with salt and pepper
2. In a large Dutch oven or heavy-bottomed stockpot
   1. **Heat 2 tablespoons butter** over medium-high heat until melted
3. Once foaming subsides and butter begins to brown
4. **Add steak** and sear
5. 2 to 3 minutes, stir occasionally, until cubes are generously browned all over
6. Transfer to a large plate
7. Set aside

### Step 2: Sauté Mushrooms

1. **Add cremini mushrooms** to pot/Dutch oven and sauté
2. ~8 minutes, stir occasionally, until mushrooms release their liquid and are deeply browned
3. Transfer to a bowl
4. Set aside

### Step 3: Sauté Aromatics (Onion, Carrot, Celery)

1. **Add**
   1. **2 tablespoons of butter**
   2. **Onions**
   3. **Carrots**
   4. **Celery**
2. ~4 minutes, **stir** until **vegetables** are softened and browned around edges
3. ~30 seconds, **add tomato paste** (1 tablespoon) and stir to incorporate
4. ~30 seconds, **add flour** (1/4 cup) and **stir** until **vegetables** are coated and no bright white streaks remain

### Step 4: Deglaze and Simmer

Do not add steak yet...

1. ~30 seconds, **Add sherry (white wine)** and stir vigorously
   - Scraping up browned bits with a wooden spoon, until fragrant and almost completely evaporated
2. Into the cooking vessel add:
   - The chicken broth with porcini mushrooms
   - Drained, canned diced tomatoes
   - Cooked cremini mushrooms
   - Steak sauce (1 tablespoon)
   - Worcestershire sauce (1 tablespoon)
   - Salt (1 teaspoon)
   - Pepper (1 teaspoon)

### Step 5: Simmer and Finish

1. Bring to a boil
2. Reduce heat to medium low and simmer
3. ~15 minutes, stirring occasionally, until soup is glossy and has thickened
4. Season with additional salt and pepper to taste
5. **Add steak** and stir just until heated through (about 1 minute)
6. Ladle into individual bowls and serve
