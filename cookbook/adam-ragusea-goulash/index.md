## 💡 Notes & Variations

- Use Instant Pot on `meat/stew` setting

## 🛒 Ingredients

### Main Components

- [ ] 1x Beef (Tri-tip, chuck, or short rib), ~2–3 lbs / 1+ kg, cut into big chunks
- [ ] 3x Fresh tomatoes, roughly chopped
- [ ] 1x Red bell pepper
- [ ] 1x Yellow or white onion
- [ ] 3x Garlic cloves
- [ ] Optional: 1x Celery stalk & celery leaves (for garnish)
- [ ] 2x Carrots, cut into thick pieces
- [ ] 1x Beef bone broth
- [ ] Egg (1, for Csipetke/pinch pasta) with a little bit of salt
- [ ] All-purpose flour (as much as egg absorbs)

## Seasonings & Pantry

- [ ] Salt & black pepper
- [ ] Olive oil
- [ ] Tomato paste
- [ ] Sweet Hungarian paprika (large handful)
- [ ] Bay leaves
- [ ] Caraway seeds (toasted)
- [ ] Fish sauce (optional, for extra umami)
- [ ] Vinegar & Sugar (optional, to balance flavor)
- [ ] Sour cream (optional garnish)

## ⏱️ Prep Work

1. Roughly chop tomatoes, red bell pepper, onion, garlic, and celery stalk
2. Puree together in a food processor with a splash of beef broth
3. Cut beef into large chunks and season heavily with salt and pepper.

### Csipetke pasta

1. Beat 1 egg with a pinch of salt, knead in flour until a smooth dough forms, and rest in the fridge for at least 20 minutes.

## 👩‍🍳 Instructions

1. Heat olive oil in a large pot and brown beef chunks in batches
2. Reduce heat, stir in tomato paste and sweet paprika, and fry briefly
3. Deglaze the pot with the pureed vegetable mixture, scraping up browned bits
4. Add the remaining beef bone broth.
5. Cover and simmer low and slow until beef is almost tender (several hours ~5 hours).
6. Add thick carrot chunks and bay leaves, simmering for 30 minutes until tender.
7. Pinch small pieces of pasta dough directly into the stew to cook through quickly.
8. Remove bay leaves and adjust flavor with salt, pepper, fish sauce, vinegar, and sugar to taste.
9. Serve topped with toasted caraway seeds and fresh celery leaves.
