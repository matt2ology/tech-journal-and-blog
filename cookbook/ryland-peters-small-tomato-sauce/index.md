## 🛒 Ingredients

### Main Components

- [ ] 1x **Large Onion**, cut into wdges

### Seasonings & Pantry

- [ ] **Extra Virgin Olive Oil** (4 tablespoons | 1/4 cup)
- [ ] 3x **Garlic cloves** - peeled and thinly sliced
- [ ] 2x **Canned Chopped Plum Tomatoes** (400 grams | 14 oz.)
- [ ] A handful **fresh basil**, and some extra to serve

---

## 🔪 Prep work

1. Peel and thinly slice 3x garlic cloves

## 👩‍🍳 Instructions (Steps)

1. **In a sauce pan add:** olive oil, garlic, onion, Canned Chopped Plum Tomatoes, and basil
2. Season well and bring to boil
3. Reduce heat
4. Gently Simmer for at least 40 minutes
