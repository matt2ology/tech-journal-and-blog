**Source:** [Serious Eats: Pollo Guisado by Octavio Peña](https://www.seriouseats.com/pollo-guisado-chicken-stew-recipe-11811773)

## Notes & Variations

- Chicken pieces needs to be pre-salted, refrigerate, uncovered, for at least 30 minutes and up to 24 hours
- If you have a large enough pot, you can include the entire chicken carcass to infuse the sauce with more chickeny flavor.
- Leftover pollo guisado can be refrigerated in an airtight container for up to 4 days or frozen for up to 3 months.

## Prep work

1. Sprinkle chicken pieces evenly all over with 5 teaspoons kosher salt
2. Transfer to a large plate
   1. Refrigerate, uncovered, for at least 30 minutes and up to 24 hours.
3. ~1 minute in a blender, blend until smooth, scraping down sides of bowl as needed; and then, set aside:
   - **Chicken stock** (1 quart | 960 ml)
   - 3x Whole Coves
   - 5x Cloves garlic (medium in size)
   - 5x Dried [árbol chiles](https://www.seriouseats.com/guide-to-mexican-chiles-8756281) (5 g), stems removed (OPTIONAL IF NO LIKE SPICE)
   - Chicken bouillon powder (1 tablespoon)
   - Dried Marjoram or Mexican oregano (1 teaspoon) - substitute with basil
   - Dried thyme (1 teaspoon)
   - Ground coriander (2 teaspoons)
   - Ground cumin (1 teaspoon)
   - Sweet paprika (2 teaspoons)
   - Tomato paste (6-ounce)
   - Whole black peppercorns (2 teaspoons)

## Instructions

1. In a large stock pot or Dutch oven, heat oil over medium.
2. **Add half of the chicken pieces**, skin-side down, in a single layer
   1. ~6 minutes cook undisturbed **until skin is light golden** and transfer to a plate
   2. Repeat with the remaining pieces of chicken.
   3. KEEP THE FOND - do not clean pot
3. **Add** to, a now-empty, pot and stir to coat in rendered chicken fat:
   - Onion
   - Tomatoes
   - Salt
4. **~2 minutes cook**, stirring frequently, **until onions begin to soften**
5. **Add** seared chicken pieces to pot
   - **If used a whole chicken carcass** add to the pot (the remaining backbone and any bones after breaking down the chicken)
6. **Add** blended sauce to pot
   - Using tongs, flip meat and submerge to ensure the seared chicken pieces is thoroughly coated
7. Bring to a simmer over medium heat and reduce to low
8. ~30 minutes simmer until white meat registers 160°F (71 °C) with an instant-read thermometer and the dark meat registers 175 to 185°F (70 to 85 °C)
9. **Transfer to a clean plate or bowl** the coated seared chicken pieces - use tongs to remove as the chicken pieces reach temperature
10. Bring, remaining sauce, to boil over high heat
11. Reduce to medium-high to maintain a simmer
12. **Add** **potatoes** and **carrots**
13. **12 to 18 minutes cook**, stirring occasionally, until vegetables are fork-tender and sauce has a velvety consistency.
14. Return coated seared chicken pieces to pot and simmer until chicken heated through
15. Season with salt to taste
16. Serve with crusty bread and cilantro.
