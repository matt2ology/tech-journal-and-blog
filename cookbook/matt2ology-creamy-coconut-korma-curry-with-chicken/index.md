  - Carrots
  - Mushrooms

## Instructions

1. Pan fry chicken thighs
2. When chicken thighs are golden brown and [fond develops in pan](https://madeincookware.com/blogs/fond-cooking)
3. Remove chicken thighs onto a plate, place into microwave to keep warm (don't turn on microwave - it's a place to keep the cooked chicken)
4. Cook mushrooms, with a little water, and let the mushrooms absorb the chicken fond
5. When mushrooms are cooked, remove and set aside (bowl, plate, wherever)
6. Heat oil in a pan over medium heat
7. Add up to 24 oz (680 G) of protein or vegetables and sauté
8. For 3-4 minutes until lightly browned
9. Stir in seasoning mix and cook for 1 minute
10. Add back in the chicken thighs and mushrooms
11. Add entire jar of korma curry sauce
    - Bury the chicken thighs under the sauce
12. Bring to a boil, then simmer for 10-15 minutes
    - OR UNTIL the protein is fully cooked and vegetables are tender
13. Add 3.5 fl. oz (100 mL) water, if desired
14. Serve hot with rice or naan.
