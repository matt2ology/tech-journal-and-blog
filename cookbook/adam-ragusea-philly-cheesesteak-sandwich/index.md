## 💡 Notes & Variations

- Add sliced avocados

## 🛒 Ingredients

### Main Components

- [ ] Ribeye steak (or a cheaper cut of meat)
- [ ] Onions (frenched)
- [ ] Mushrooms (optional)
- [ ] Soft hoagie rolls

### Seasonings & Pantry

- [ ] Oil (for cooking)
- [ ] Vinegar or lime juice
- [ ] Mild cheddar cheese (or Jack / Colby)
- [ ] Heavy cream
- [ ] Lime juice (for the cheese sauce)
- [ ] Baking soda (sodium bicarb)
- [ ] Pickled or canned peppers (optional, for the cheese sauce)
- [ ] Salt (to taste)

---

## 🔪 Prep work

1. **Freeze the steak** until firm (about 2 hours) so it can be sliced paper-thin.
2. **French the onions** (trim the blossom end, cut straight toward the core without cutting through the root).
   1. Take the half - trim the blossom end
   2. From the blossom orient the knife towards the root end, but not to it (the root holds it all together)
   3. Cut as thickly or thinly (I prefer thin)
   4. When done release the stringy slice strips by trimming off the root end
3. **Slice mushrooms** (optional)
4. Finely **chop** pickled or canned **peppers**.
5. Slice the partially frozen steak against the grain into very thin pieces (about a millimeter thick).

## 👩‍🍳 Instructions (Steps)

1. **Cook the onions:** Heat oil in a wide pan over high heat
   1. Add the onions and cook while stirring almost constantly
   2. Deglaze the pan with a little bit of water if fond sticks to the bottom
   3. Cook until soft and caramelized (amber colored), then move them to one side of the pan
2. **Cook the mushrooms (optional)** In the empty side of the pan
   1. Add a little olive oil and cook the mushrooms until nicely colored
   2. then set them aside
3. **Make the cheese sauce:** In a small pot
   1. Combine fresh lime juice (about 2 teaspoons) and baking soda (1/4 teaspoon)
   2. Let it react and stop foaming
   3. Then stir in heavy cream (about half of a cup)
   4. Over moderate heat, slowly melt in the grated mild cheddar cheese a little at a time until smooth
   5. Stir in the finely chopped peppers and keep over low heat
4. **Cook the steak:** Heat oil in a heavy skillet (like cast iron) over high heat until it starts to smoke
   1. Lay in the thin steak slices, getting some color on them without overcooking or making them leathery
   2. Move them to a cooler side of the pan as you go
   3. Deglaze the pan with a tiny bit of water to coat the meat in the browned bits
   4. Then remove from heat and reheat the onions and mushrooms.
5. **Assemble the sandwich:** Slice open a soft hoagie roll
   1. Layer with caramelized onions, mushrooms (if using), a generous amount of the homemade cheese sauce, and the cooked steak slices (with extra cheese sauce on top if desired).
