## 🛒 Ingredients

### Main Components

- [ ] 1-2 shallots
- [ ] 10-12 oz (approx. 300g) salmon fillet meat
- [ ] Burgers buns

### Seasonings & Pantry

- [ ] Breadcrumbs
- [ ] Butter
- [ ] Fresh dill
- [ ] Honey
- [ ] Lemon juice
- [ ] Mustard
- [ ] Pepper
- [ ] Salt

---

## 🔪 Prep work

1. Finely chop shallots
2. Finely chop dill
3. Both shallots and dill put in a mixing bowl along with:
    1. A spoonful of mustard
    2. Squeeze of lemon juice (a teaspoon, max)
    3. A small squeeze of honey
    4. Few grinds of pepper.
4. Remove the skin from the salmon
5. Dice the salmon into approx. 1/4 inch (6mm) cubes
6. Put 2/3 of the fish into the mixing bowl
7. Chop the remaining third as fine as you can
    1. Grind it into the cutting board with the side of your knife to make a fine paste
    2. Add the remaining finely chopped and fine paste third to the bowl
8. Mix everything with just enough breadcrumbs, maybe a quarter cup (30g), to make it moldable
    1. Mold into two patties that match the size and shape of your burger buns
    2. Season the outside of the patties with salt just prior to cooking.
    3. Unlike beef patties, they won't shrink much during cooking.

## 👩‍🍳 Instructions (Steps)

1. Heat a pan (preferably nonstick) on medium-low heat and melt in some butter
2. When the butter is just starting to brown, lay in the patties
3. Cook until the first side is brown and flip
4. Push on the surface of the burger
    1. When you feel the inside go a little firm, they're done
    2. Usually takes six minutes, total
5. Put the patty on the bun and top with [[../adam-ragusea-slaw-for-salmon-burgers/index|slaw]]
