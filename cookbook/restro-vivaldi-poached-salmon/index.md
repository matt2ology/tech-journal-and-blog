## 💡 Notes & Variations

- Add any mushroom - fry in pan with water, then brown off with oil

## 🛒 Ingredients

### Main Components

- 8 oz salmon fillet (skin removed)
- 1/4 cup sliced scallions
- 1/4 cup sliced leeks
- Handful of baby spinach
- 1//2 cup chicken, veg, or fish stock
- 1/4 cup dry white wine
- 1 cup heavy cream

### Seasonings & Pantry

- Salt and pepper to taste

---

## 👩‍🍳 Instructions

1. Put all ingredients in a frying pan at once (with salmon on top) and cover with a lid
2. Bring to a boil and simmer salmon to desired doneness
3. Remove salmon at desired doneness and reduce sauce till thickened
4. Pour sauce over salmon
