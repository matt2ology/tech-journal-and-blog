## 💡 Notes & Variations

- Can use any mushrooms for fun like king trumpets, lions mane, oyster, beech, or shiitake

## 🛒 Ingredients

### Main Components

- [ ] Beef Ribeye Steak Boneless, Thin Cut (Kirkland Signature USDA Choice)
- [ ] Corn or flour tortillas
- [ ] Sour cream
- [ ] Bell peppers: Traffic light (Red, Yellow, Green)

For use in guacamole and as topping for the tacos:

- [ ] Avocados
- [ ] Cilantro
- [ ] Jalapeño (optional)
- [ ] Lime
- [ ] Mushrooms (optional)
- [ ] Onions: Yellow, sweet, or white
- [ ] Tomatoes

### Seasonings & Pantry

- [ ] Salt & pepper

---

## 🔪 Prep work

- **Prepare the Toppings:** Dice the bell peppers, onions, tomatoes, and any other desired toppings.

### Guacamole

Optional Additions / Variations:

- [ ] Garlic
- [ ] Spices (e.g., ground cumin)

1. **Mash the Avocados:** Use a molcajete or fork 2. Halve and pit the avocados 3. Scoop the flesh into a molcajete (or mixing bowl) and mash to your preferred consistency chunkier or smoother depending on taste
2. **Prep & Add Produce:** Dice evenly for uniform distribution 5. Dice the tomatoes, jalapeño (optional), onion, and cilantro 6. Add them directly to the mashed avocado base
3. **Season and Lime:** Adjust acidity and salt to taste 8. Squeeze fresh lime juice over the mixture and sprinkle with salt to enhance the flavor
4. **Mix and Serve:** Combine all ingredients thoroughly and serve immediately 10. Stir everything together until well combined 11. Taste and adjust salt or lime juice if needed

## 👩‍🍳 Instructions (Steps)

1. **Pan fry the toppings:** Heat a pan over medium heat, add a small amount of oil or butter, and sauté the diced bell peppers, onions, and any other desired toppings until they are tender and slightly caramelized.
   - Use a little water as an interface when cooking mushrooms, so to collapse their porous, spongy structure first so they don't absorb excessive oil or burn during browning
2. **Cook the Ribeye Steak**:
   - In a hot pan, flipping every 30 seconds, to your desired level of doneness
   - OR: in an air fryer cook the ribeye steak
3. **Slice the Ribeye Steak:** Once the ribeye steak is cooked, on a salted and peppered cutting board, slice it into thin strips for easier assembly of the tacos
4. **Assemble the Tacos:** Warm the tortillas, then fill them with the cooked ribeye steak, guacamole, and any additional toppings of your choice.
