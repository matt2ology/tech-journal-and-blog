## Notes & Variations

- I like salmon skin, so I left the salmon as is (with skin)
- I used Caraway (Carum carvi) instead of ground cumin
- Instead of making harissa paste - just mixed honey and sriracha (to taste)
- I just used the default fish preset on the air fryer

## Ingredients

### Main Components

- [ ] **Avocado** slices for serving (optional)
- [ ] **Fresh lemon juice** (2 tablespoons | 30 ml)
- [ ] **Skinless salmon fillet**, cut into 1 1/2-inch pieces (16 ounces | 454 g)
- [ ] 1/2 **large English cucumber** (10 ounces; 285 g), halved lengthwise and sliced into very thin half-moons
- [ ] 2x medium **carrots** (2 ounces; 656 g each), **peeled**
- [ ] Cooked **rice** (4 cups)

### Seasonings & Pantry

- [ ] **Diamond Crystal kosher salt**, divided; for table salt, use half as much by volume (1/2 teaspoons)
- [ ] **Extra-virgin olive oil** (2 tablespoons | 30 ml)
- [ ] **Fresh dill fronds**, chopped, plus more for garnish (1/4 cup | 6 g)
- [ ] **Freshly ground black pepper**, divided (3/4 teaspoon)
- [ ] **Ground cumin** (1/2 teaspoon)
- [ ] **Harissa paste** (6 tablespoons | 90 ml)
- [ ] **Honey** (4 tablespoons | 60 ml)
- [ ] 4x medium **cloves garlic**, finely minced or grated on a Microplane grater

---

## Prep work

1. **Cook rice** (wash rice)
2. **Garlic**, finely minced or grated on a Microplane grater
3. Shave **carrots** lengthwise into ribbons, rotating carrot as needed
   - Use a Y-peeler; or, simply chop as thinly as can
4. Halve **cucumber** lengthwise and sliced into very thin half-moons

## Instructions

### For the Salmon

1. **In a small bowl**, whisk to **combine**
   - **Harissa** (6 tablespoons | 90 ml)
   - **Honey** (4 tablespoons | 60 ml)
   - **Extra-virgin olive oil** (2 tablespoons | 30 ml)
   - Minced/grated **Garlic**
   - **Ground cumin** (1/2 teaspoon)
   - **Salt** (1 teaspoon)
   - **Pepper** (1/2 teaspoon)
2. **Transfer 1/4 cup harissa sauce** to a medium bowl
   1. Add **salmon**, gently tossing to coat
   2. Set aside while air fryer preheats
   3. Reserve remaining harissa sauce for serving
3. Preheat 6-quart air fryer at 400°F (205 °C) for 5 minutes
   1. Add **marinated salmon** to air fryer basket in a single layer
   2. Set to fish preset, or Cook until caramelized around the edges and just cooked through, 5 to 8 minutes

### For the Carrot and Cucumber Salad

In a large bowl, toss to combine:

- **Carrots** ribbons
- **Cucumber** half-moons
- Chopped **Dill**
- **Lemon juice** (2 tablespoons | 30 ml)
- **Salt** (1/2 teaspoon)
- **Pepper** (1/4 teaspoon)
