## 💡 Notes & Variations

- This is a condiment for a burger, so it should taste too strong on its own
- Ideally let it sit in the fridge for a few hours (or overnight) before using

## 🛒 Ingredients

### Main Components

- [ ] Bagged pre-cut slaw mix
- [ ] Chopped cilantro

### Seasonings & Pantry

- [ ] Celery seeds (very optional)
- [ ] Mayonnaise
- [ ] Salt
- [ ] Soy sauce
- [ ] Sugar
- [ ] Vinegar (or like rice vinegar)

---

## 👩‍🍳 Instructions (Steps)

1. Mix the veg with a big glug of vinegar and just enough mayo to hold it together
2. Stir in a big pinch of sugar and add:
   1. A small pinch of salt
   2. Some celery seeds (if using)
   3. A splash of soy sauce + chopped cilantro (if using), and taste
3. Taste and adjust the seasoning
