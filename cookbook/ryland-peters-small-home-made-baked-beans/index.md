## 🛒 Ingredients

### Main Components

- [ ] 1 Onion, chopped
- [ ] Bread, toast
- [ ] Fresh Parmesan Cheese, to serve

### Seasonings & Pantry

- [ ] Extra Virgin Olive Oil (2 tablespoons)
- [ ] Dried Chili / Red Pepper Flakes (1/2 teaspoon)
- [ ] Canned Butter / Lima Beans (800 grams; or, 1 & 3/4 cups) - rinsed and drained
- [ ] [[../ryland-peters-small-tomato-sauce/index|Tomato Sauce]]
- [ ] Sea Salt
- [ ] Black Pepper (fresh ground)

## 🔪 Prep work

1. ![[../ryland-peters-small-tomato-sauce/index#👩‍🍳 Instructions (Steps)|Tomato sauce]]
2. Rinsed and drained canned Butter / Lima Beans

## 👩‍🍳 Instructions (Steps)

1. Heat oil in a saucepan
2. Gently fry onion and chili/hot red pepper flakes for 10 minutes (until softened, but not golden)
3. Add Butter / Lima Beans
4. Stir once (1 time) in the saucepan
5. Add tomato sauce
