## 💡 Notes & Variations

- I used chicken stock
- I added
  - Spinach to the soup after blending and when in the pot to warm-up
  - I pan-fried some sliced white button mushrooms as "toppings"
  - In a small sandwich zip-lock bag - I crushed peanuts for topping
  - Drizzled coconut milk for style and contrast

## 🛒 Ingredients

### Main Components

- [ ] 1 medium butternut squash, halved + seeds removed
- [ ] 1 red onion, cut in half
- [ ] 1 red bell pepper
- [ ] 4 carrots, peeled
- [ ] 3 cups cherry tomatoes

### Seasonings & Pantry

- [ ] 2-4 tbsp avocado oil
- [ ] 2 heads garlic
- [ ] 1 tsp salt
- [ ] 1 tsp paprika
- [ ] 1 tsp pepper
- [ ] 1 tsp thyme
- [ ] 2 cups vegetable broth, plus more if needed

---

## 🔪 Prep work

1. Preheat oven to 425°F
2. Place squash cut-side up (the long way) on a baking tray
3. Add the onion halves into the squash cavities
4. Add the bell pepper, carrots, garlic and cherry tomatoes to the tray
5. Drizzle everything with oil
6. Season with salt, paprika, pepper and thyme, ensuring to rub the seasoning into the squash

## 👩‍🍳 Instructions (Steps)

1. Roast for 50-60 minutes, or until everything is soft and caramelized
2. Scoop the squash out of the skin and add everything from the tray to a blender, including the roasted garlic and juices
3. Add the vegetable broth and blend until completely smooth
4. Warm the soup up however you like and adjust seasonings as you see fit
5. Serve with chili oil, a drizzle of coconut milk, fresh herbs and lots of toasted sourdough. Enjoy
