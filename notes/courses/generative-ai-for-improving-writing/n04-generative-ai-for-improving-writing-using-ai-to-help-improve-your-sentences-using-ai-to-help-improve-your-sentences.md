### iduQCmLPZin1

> [!summary] Cumulative Summary
> This module will focus on the micro-level focusing on the words uses than that of the organization and key points of the document.

<!-- blockquote separator -->

> [!quote]
> In this module, we're focusing on local sentence level revisions, rather than more global or document‑level revisions.

<!-- blockquote separator -->

> [!note] Marginalia / Reflection
> Large Language Model (LLM) GenAI is a fancy text generator chat bot. If it had any good use it would receive input, mutate or iterate, to help articulate the user's intent where skill and ability does not meet the desired effect.
>
> - **Local revisions (sentence-level)**
>   - **Fixing grammar, punctuation, and typos** (all things a text editor may already do for you)
>   - **Tightening wordy or redundant sentences** (if you need an LLM to do this what is your worth)
>     - Refer to "Shitty First Drafts" by Anne Lamott from Bird by Bird
>   - **Adjusting tone** (friendlier, more formal, more neutral)
>     - You are a fake and you know it, why are you losing your personal tone and style to let AI speak for you
>     - You can still, genuinely and with integrity, write to be more friendlier, more formal, more neutral in your own words.
>     - If anyone perceives it otherwise what makes you think that having AI write it for you will prevent it happening regardless?
>   - **Improving clarity in a single sentence**
>     - You work in an corporate/office environment, presumably a college degree, yet did not build up the skills to simplify subject, objects, and verbs nor have the ability to abstract key points or arguments to a single bullet point?
>   - **Choosing more precise or specific wording**
>     - Why do you need to pre-optimize or over complicate things?
>     - If it's not of your vocabulary set or natural way of speaking you are absolutely denying your own voice and writing style
>     - If the end product is supposed to be for a targeted audience should you not already know the respective jargon or phrases required to communicate
>     - In common written communication or presentation it is of the same language within the organization - people talk to people, so why is it a priority to make AI talk to people - GIVE ME A LIVE HUMAN REPRESENTATIVE!
> - **Global revisions (document-level)**
>   - **Reorganizing sections or paragraphs for better flow**
>     - Can you not walkthrough an outline or know your own content to see that for yourself?
>   - **Revising the overall structure or outline**
>     - Again what does AI have to offer that you can do it yourself quicker than it takes to prompt, find the documents, wait for it to upload, and then re-prompt if not in the format that you already envisioned
>   - **Strengthening the purpose, or central message**
>     - If you don't know your own arguments, key points, or central message what value do you have to offer to begin with
>     - Do you not have critical thinking or reasoning skills that you need someone else to think for you?
>   - **Adding or removing entire sections to improve coherence**
>     - Can you not read your own writing to do this yourself?
>     - Do you not have ownership or autonomy to do this?
>     - Is knowing your own output too exhaustive to remove unnecessary points and arguments to improve coherence and readability?

| Local revisions (sentence-level)                       | Global revisions (document-level)                         |
| ------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| Fixing grammar, punctuations, and typos                | Reorganizing sections or paragraphs for better flow       |
| Tightening wordy or redundant sentences                | Revising the overall structure or outline                 |
| Adjusting tone (friendlier, more formal, more neutral) | Strengthening the purpose, or central message             |
| Improving clarity in a sentence                        | Adding or removing entire selections to improve coherence |
| Choosing more precise or specific wording              |                                                           |

### id6u467fWUf7

> [!summary] Cumulative Summary
> AI can help write at the micro-level focusing on the words than that of the organization and key points by way of "optimizing" both the words used and adjusting your ideas.
>
> Feed your original sentence into the AI tool, prompt it by request to revise to sound professional and keep original meaning, and AI will modify your voice and style.

<!-- blockquote separator -->

> [!quote]
> "Globomantics will utilize innovative strategies for maximizing internal communication efficiency." Now, that sentence is grammatically correct, but it's not necessarily what I'd call a good sentence. It's stiff and full of corporate‑sounding jargon. Well, AI can help fix that. We can enter our original sentence and a prompt, asking the AI to make it more concise or friendly or professional while keeping the meaning. For example, we could write, "Revise this sentence to make it clear and concise for a professional proposal, but keep my meaning." So entering those two things, that original sentence and the prompt, into any AI tool of your choice, it might return something like this. "Globomantics will use new strategies to improve internal communication."

<!-- blockquote separator -->

> [!note] Marginalia / Reflection
> **Original sentence:** Globomantics will utilize innovative strategies for maximizing internal communication efficiency."
>
> **Prompt:** "Revise this sentence to make it clear and concise for a professional proposal, but keep my meaning."
>
> At what point would anything written would be original to the author: refer to "Theseus’s Paradox".

### idWPHU7cerK5

> [!summary] Cumulative Summary
> AI can aid not just in the macro (outlining), but also in the micro by prompting the AI to revise your original voice and style with a full rewrite.
>
> AI needs a goal when revising your original input, so Ackmann suggest that one uses action verbs (e.g. clarify, tighten, soften) to orient the final output.

<!-- blockquote separator -->

> [!quote]
> When you revise with AI, use action verbs as shown here. Words like clarify, tighten, soften, to stay in control of the final meaning that the AI outputs.

<!-- blockquote separator -->

> [!note] Marginalia / Reflection
> Prompt AI with Action Verbs by way of "clarity" (e.g. clarify, simplify, make specific, etc.) "concision" (e.g. tighten, condense, remove filler, etc.), and "tone" (e.g. soften, make friendly, make professional, etc.).

| Clarity       | Concision         | Tone              |
| ------------- | ----------------- | ----------------- |
| Clarify       | Tighten           | Soften            |
| Explain       | Shorten           | Formalize         |
| Simplify      | Condense          | Make friendly     |
| Rephrase      | Streamline        | Make professional |
| Make specific | Remove filler     | Adjust tone       |
| Make clearer  | Reduce redundancy | Make neutral      |

### idmrKKTmp0bm

> [!summary] Cumulative Summary
> When using AI to help with your writing at a micro-level (the words you use in your writing) give it a goal by prompting with action verbs (e.g. clarify, tighten, soften, etc.), so that AI can attempt to rewrite your original sentence with those outcomes.
>
> Ackmann reminds users, for the sake of privacy, to generalize and remove sensitive figures; and, due to copyright laws one must not simply copy-and-paste, but also review and revise the implemented suggestions given by AI.

<!-- blockquote separator -->

> [!quote]
> always remove names and any business confidential details before prompting. And copyright reminder, under U.S. Copyright Office and EU IPO guidelines, you maintain authorship only when you decide what stays. AI suggestions become part of your writing only after your human review and revision of the output. Never just copy and paste whatever AI gives you without reading through it thoroughly.
