> 
> Again this is not a problem of writing.
> It is a problem of scope, and having clear understanding and expectations to execute and deliver good quality work product.
> 
> When writing why is there no consideration of:
> - Separation of concerns - is it technical, policy, executive, customer oriented? 
> - Principle of least privilege - who really needs to know
> 
> Why would shipping and receiving need to care about an office memo that people should be mindful if they are wearing too strong of a perfume when on-site.
